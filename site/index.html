What the platform handles

Designed around real school assessment workflows rather than a simple online quiz.

Question Bank

MCQ, true/false, multiple-select, short answer and numeric questions organised by subject, grade and difficulty.

Exam Scheduling

Assign exams to classes or individual students, define opening/closing times, duration and pass thresholds.

Secure Attempts

Server-owned deadlines, autosaved answers and protected answer keys that never need to be exposed before submission.

Role Access

Separate student, teacher, academic administrator and super administrator capabilities.

Results

Server-side marking with controlled result publication and student-facing score views.

Auditability

Administrative actions and account activity can be logged for accountability and troubleshooting.
